Meri galti thi.

Maine ek ladki ke post pe comment kiya. Mere liye woh do second ki baat thi — par tumne dekha, aur tumhe dukh hua. Aur ab mujhe sirf wahi dikh raha hai.

Jab tumne pucha, mera pehla reaction khud ko bachana tha. "Aisa kuch nahi hai", "tum galat samajh rahi ho". Woh sabse bura part tha. Tumhe apni feeling prove karni padi — aur wo tumhe kabhi nahi karni chahiye.

Tum overreact nahi kar rahi thi. Main hi bewakoof tha.

"Ye nahi karunga, wo nahi karunga" — bolna sabse easy cheez hai, aur tum ye sab pehle bhi sun chuki ho. Main tumhe promise ki list nahi dena chahta.

Main bas itna chahta hoon ki tumhe dubara ye feel na ho ki tum kisi se compete kar rahi ho. Kabhi bhi. Kisi se bhi.

Aur agar kabhi maine phir se kuch aisa kiya jo tumhe chubhe — toh main bahana nahi banaunga. Main pehle sun'unga. Ye wala part main sach mein badal raha hoon.
